About

Research-minded, animal-focused.

I am an animal scientist with a background in dairy science, animal genetics and agricultural data analysis. My current work focuses on bovine milk microbiomes and how methodological choices can affect microbiome-based assessment of mastitis risk.

I combine biological questions with quantitative approaches, from molecular methods and 16S rRNA sequencing to statistical analysis and microbial diversity profiling.

Research

Current and recent work

MASTER'S THESIS · ONGOING

Towards Reliable Bovine Milk Microbiome Analysis for Assessing Mastitis Risk through Comparative Evaluation of Two DNA Extraction Methods

This project examines how DNA extraction methods may influence bovine milk microbiome analysis. The work uses 16S rRNA sequencing data, R-based bioinformatic processing and MicrobiomeAnalyst 2.0 for microbial profiling and diversity analysis.

16S rRNA Milk microbiome Mastitis R MicrobiomeAnalyst
RESEARCH PROJECT · 2025

Variation in Mineral Content of Forages in the USA

Large-scale comparative data modelling using R to evaluate regional forage variation, with applications to precision nutrition and dairy mineral supplementation.

Sponsor: ANIMINE, France
